Co-authored paper published in *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*: **ExoMol line lists – LXIII. ExoMol line lists for 12 isotopologues of CO₂**.

The paper presents extensive rovibrational line lists for 12 CO₂ isotopologues, with quantum number assignments supplemented by machine-learning-based estimators — a direct output of my PhD research at UCL ExoMol. The resulting data are available at [www.exomol.com](https://www.exomol.com) and have been used to generate opacities with four radiative transfer codes (TauREx, ARCiS, NEMESIS, petitRADTRANS).

**Citation:** Yurchenko, Barnfield *et al.*, *MNRAS* 545(3), staf2135 (2025) — [doi:10.1093/mnras/staf2135](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf2135)
