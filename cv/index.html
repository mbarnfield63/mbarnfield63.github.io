Professional Summary

Third-year PhD researcher in the ExoMol group at University College London. I build machine learning tools for large molecular spectroscopy databases, used to model the atmospheres of exoplanets. I published two first-author papers in 2026, in the Journal of Molecular Spectroscopy and in JQSRT.

Experience

  • 2024 - present

    London, UK

    University College London, Department of Physics and Astronomy
    Supervisors: Prof. Jonathan Tennyson FRS and Prof. Sergei N. Yurchenko
    • Published a first-author paper on neural network corrections to the energy levels of rare isotopic molecules (Journal of Molecular Spectroscopy, 2026).
